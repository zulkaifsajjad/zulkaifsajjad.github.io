## Abstract
Objective: To design and validate a fully automated, feature-agnostic deep learning approach for the early detection of glaucoma from fundus images. 

Method: The retrospective study was conducted at Al-Shifa Trust Eye Hospital, Rawalpindi, Pakistan, using a dataset of 1,200 retinal fundus images. A deep convolutional neural network was developed without requiring explicit segmentation of the optic disc or cup. The model achieved high sensitivity and specificity in distinguishing glaucomatous from normal eyes, presenting an efficient clinical screening tool.
