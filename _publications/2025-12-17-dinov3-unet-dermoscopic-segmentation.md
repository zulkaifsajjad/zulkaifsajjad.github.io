## Abstract
Accurate skin lesion segmentation plays a vital role in diagnosis and serves as a fundamental step in the clinical decision-making pipeline. Although CNN-based models are widely used and effective in capturing local representations, they often struggle to model long-range dependencies and global context. Recent hybrid approaches that combine CNNs with Transformers attempt to address this limitation but still fail to achieve efficient fusion between local and global features. 

To overcome these challenges, we propose a unified DINOv3-UNet encoder-decoder architecture that incorporates a novel Channel Attention Adapter (CAA) to enhance feature fusion and segmentation accuracy.
