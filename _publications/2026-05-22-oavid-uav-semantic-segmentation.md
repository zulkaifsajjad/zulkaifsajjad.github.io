## Abstract
Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) have become a tool of trade in data acquisition due to their ability to capture high-definition images. However, commonly available UAV datasets such as UAVid and VDD are captured in nadir view, limiting their efficacy for oblique perspectives. 

To bridge this gap, this paper introduces OAVid, a comprehensive benchmark dataset for semantic segmentation of oblique aerial images captured by low-altitude UAVs. Baseline benchmarks using state-of-the-art segmentation networks demonstrate the challenges and utility of the proposed dataset.
