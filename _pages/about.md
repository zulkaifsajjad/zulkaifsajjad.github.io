Hi, I am **Zulkaif Sajjad**, a researcher and electrical engineering student at the **University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Taxila**. 

My research primarily focuses on **Medical Image Analysis**, **Surgical AI**, and **Explainable AI (XAI)**. I work on designing advanced deep learning architectures, vision transformers (such as DINOv2 and DINOv3), and lightweight parameter-efficient adapters to address challenging real-world problems in medical segmentation, automated disease detection (e.g., glaucoma screening and dermoscopic lesion analysis), and cross-domain deepfake detection.

Feel free to explore my [Publications](/publications/) and connect with me on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=AE9hzbgAAAAJ&hl=en), [GitHub](https://github.com/zulkaifsajjad), or [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Zulkaif-Sajjad).

## Research Interests
